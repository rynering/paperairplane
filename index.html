BEFORE YOU TAKE OFF

Good flights start
with good folds.

A little care on the ground goes a long way in the air.

01

Keep it crisp.

Line up the edges before pressing each crease firmly. Symmetry helps your plane fly straight.

02

Easy does it.

Start with a gentle, level launch in an open space. Aim away from faces, animals, and fragile things.

03

Test. Tweak. Try again.

If it dives, bend the back edges up a tiny bit. If it climbs and stalls, flatten them or bend them slightly down.
